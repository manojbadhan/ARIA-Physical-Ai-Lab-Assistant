## 1. Short answer for the robot

This is most likely a **Creality Wash and Cure Machine**, a post-processing device used with **resin 3D printers**. It has two main functions:
1. **Wash:** helps remove uncured liquid resin from a resin-printed model using a compatible cleaning liquid.
2. **Cure:** uses ultraviolet (UV) light to further harden a washed and dried resin print.

It is **not itself a 3D printer**. It normally works after a compatible resin printer has made the object.

## 2. What is it used for?

Resin prints often have uncured resin left on their surfaces after printing. Washing removes much of this residue. UV curing then helps the resin reach its intended final properties. The exact washing liquid, washing time, curing time, and compatibility depend on the resin and the machine manual.

Typical applications:
- Post-processing small resin 3D-printed models and prototypes.
- Cleaning a print in a basket, or in some setups cleaning a print while it remains attached to its build plate.
- UV post-curing after washing and drying.

It is generally intended for resin-printing workflows, not for cleaning ordinary household objects or filament-printed parts.

## 3. How does it work?

### Wash mode
The machine agitates the cleaning liquid in its washing container. The liquid flows around the model and helps loosen/remove uncured resin from its surface. A basket or compatible build-plate holder may be used, depending on the model and supplied accessories.

### Cure mode
The washing container is removed and the curing platform is installed, according to the model’s instructions. UV LEDs illuminate the washed, fully dried resin model. A rotating platform, where fitted, helps expose different sides to the UV light.

**Important:** Washing and curing are different steps. Do not put a wet model straight into curing unless the resin and manufacturer instructions explicitly allow it.

## 6. General operating guide

This is a **general guide for the likely UW-01 family**, not a substitute for the exact model’s manual. Confirm the model and accessories first.

### Before starting
1. Put the machine on a stable, level surface in a ventilated work area.
2. Read the resin and cleaning-liquid safety information. Wear suitable gloves and eye protection when handling uncured resin or contaminated cleaning liquid.
3. Keep uncured resin and cleaning liquid off skin and away from food, children, and pets.
4. Check that the power adapter, cable, cover, container, and accessories are undamaged.
5. Use only a cleaning liquid compatible with the resin and machine. Do not pour liquid into the main base or electronics.

### A. Washing workflow
1. Ensure the machine is switched off before arranging the container and accessories.
2. Put the compatible washing container in place and add the manufacturer-approved cleaning liquid as directed by the manual. Do not overfill.
3. Place the resin print in the supplied washing basket, or use the supported build-plate holder if the exact model supports it.
4. Close/fit the protective cover.
5. Power on the unit, select **Wash**, and choose a suitable time and speed according to the resin instructions and model manual.
6. Press **Start** and allow the cycle to finish. Do not open or reach into a moving machine.
7. Remove the model carefully and let it dry fully in a suitable ventilated area before curing.

### B. Curing workflow
1. Switch off/pause as directed by the manual and remove the washing container.
2. Install the correct curing platform/turntable supplied for the machine.
3. Place the washed and fully dried resin print on the platform, arranged so it is stable.
4. Fit the orange protective cover.
5. Select **Cure**, then set the time and speed according to the resin manufacturer’s instructions and the machine manual.
6. Press **Start** and keep the cover fitted during operation.
7. When the cycle finishes, allow the model to cool if needed and check whether additional curing is required by the resin instructions.

**Do not use a generic curing time as a universal rule.** Resin type, object size, geometry, colour, and wall thickness affect the required curing.

## 7. Safety precautions

- Never look directly at UV LEDs or operate curing mode without the protective cover.
- Do not bypass a cover/safety interlock.
- Follow the resin Safety Data Sheet (SDS) and the cleaning-liquid label.
- Uncured resin and contaminated liquid should not be poured into a sink or drain. Follow local hazardous-waste instructions.
- Many alcohol-based cleaning liquids are flammable. Keep them away from flames, sparks, heaters, and hot surfaces; follow the product label.
- Keep the machine dry externally. Never immerse the base or wash it by pouring water over it.
- Disconnect power before cleaning, inspecting, or changing accessories.
- Do not operate if the cable, adapter, cover, or housing is damaged, or if there is an unusual smell, smoke, excessive heat, or abnormal noise.
- Keep the work area ventilated and follow the school/lab supervisor’s rules.

## 8. Basic troubleshooting

| Symptom | Possible checks |
|---|---|
| Machine does not start | Check the wall outlet, adapter connection, and power input using the manual. Do not open the electrical housing. |
| Timer/display is blank | Confirm power supply and cable are connected correctly; stop using it if the adapter or cable looks damaged. |
| Wash result is poor | Check whether the cleaning liquid is compatible and still suitable, the model is fully exposed to liquid, the basket/container is installed correctly, and the wash time follows resin guidance. |
| Model looks cloudy/white after washing | Possible causes include residue, unsuitable/contaminated cleaning liquid, or curing before the model is fully dry. Check resin guidance and the manual. |
| Abnormal noise or strong vibration | Stop the cycle, disconnect power, and check that the unit is level and the container/platform is fitted correctly. If it persists, ask the lab supervisor or service technician. |
| Curing seems uneven | Check that the correct platform is fitted, the model is positioned stably, the cover is in place, and the resin-specific curing guidance is followed. |

Do not dismantle the machine or attempt internal electrical repairs as a student operator.
